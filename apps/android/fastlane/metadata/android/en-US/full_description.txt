AI Voice Trainer helps language learners practice short spoken and typed turns with concise correction-focused feedback.

The Android app uses native Android controls, keeps the OpenAI API key on the server, and connects only to the shared AI Voice Trainer backend.

Features:
- Practice short speaking turns.
- Read concise correction feedback.
- Use free chat for normal questions.
- Choose when the app is allowed to speak.
- Keep green correction results quiet.
- Test microphone, speaker, backend, and realtime connection behavior from a native debug surface.

AI Voice Trainer is designed for practice feedback, not official language assessment.
